About Me

Renee

Founder & Nail Technician

My Background

Welcome to Renee's Nail Haven! I started this business out of a deep-seated love for nail artistry and a commitment to helping clients express their personal style. With hours of dedicated training and hands-on experience, I treat every hand as a canvas, focusing on nail health, durability, and trendsetting details.

My Skills

I specialize in a wide array of professional nail service enhancements. My technical toolkit includes precise Gel Overlays, sculpted Polygel Extensions, classic structural French Tips, clean and relaxing Pedicures, quick and safe Soak-Off removals, and intricate, custom hand-painted 3D Nail Art Designs tailored completely to your aesthetic vision.
